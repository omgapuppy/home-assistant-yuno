# Brand assets

The Yuno Energy icon comes from the favicon published on the official website:
https://yunoenergy.ie/sites/default/files/Yuno_Apple%20Icon%20AW_RGB_Light.png

Retrieved 5 November 2026. The surrounding whitespace was trimmed and the mark
was centered on a square white canvas, then resized to 256 and 512 pixels for
Home Assistant. No Home Assistant branding is used.
